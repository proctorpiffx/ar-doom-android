# AR Doom — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## Overview

AR Doom is an augmented reality game that uses your device's camera to place game enemies in your real environment. We take your privacy seriously.

## Camera Access

AR Doom uses your device's camera exclusively for augmented reality gameplay. The camera feed is processed in real-time on your device to detect surfaces and track your position. **Camera data is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared.**

## ARCore / Google Services

AR Doom uses Google's ARCore framework. ARCore may collect anonymous spatial mapping data to improve AR quality, handled by Google under their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Collection

**AR Doom does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** We do not collect names, emails, location, camera recordings, analytics, or device identifiers.

## Local Data Storage

All game progress is stored locally on your device and never uploaded. Uninstalling removes all data.

## Advertising

No advertisements. No ad SDKs included.

## In-App Purchases

None. The game is completely free.

## Network Access

No internet connection required. No network requests during gameplay.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — AR gameplay (real-time, never stored)
- **Vibrate** — Haptic feedback
- **Wake Lock** — Keeps screen on during play

## Children's Privacy

Rated Mature 17+ for fantasy violence. Not intended for children under 17.
