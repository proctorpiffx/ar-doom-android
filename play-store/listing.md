## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**AR DOOM — Augmented Reality Shooter**

The demons have broken through to YOUR world. Your phone is your weapon. Your room is the battlefield.

AR DOOM uses augmented reality (ARCore) to project classic DOOM enemies into your real environment. Imps spawn on your coffee table. Demons charge from your hallway. Cacodemons float through your ceiling. You physically move, aim your phone, and blast them before they reach you.

**How to play:**
• Hold your phone up — the camera shows your real surroundings
• DOOM enemies spawn on detected surfaces around you
• TAP to fire your weapon at enemies in front of you
• Physically walk and turn to track enemies in all directions
• Enemies chase you through real space — back up, dodge, fight
• Survive waves — each wave brings more and tougher demons
• Swipe left/right or long-press to cycle weapons
• Swipe down to pick up ammo

**Enemy types:**
• Soldiers — weak but fast, attack in groups
• Imps — balanced, fast movers, dangerous up close
• Demons (Pinkies) — tanky, heavy hitters, charge attacks
• Cacodemons — flying, ranged attackers
• Barons of Hell — bosses, massive health and damage

**Weapons:**
• Pistol — reliable starter weapon
• Shotgun — high damage, slow fire rate
• Chaingun — rapid fire, lower per-shot damage
• Plasma Rifle — energy weapon, high damage
• BFG — the big one. Clears everything in front of you

**Features:**
• Full 6-DOF AR tracking — walk around your space to play
• Wave-based survival mode with escalating difficulty
• 5 enemy types with unique AI and stats
• 5 weapons to unlock and cycle through
• Haptic feedback on firing, hits, and damage
• DOOM-style HUD with health, ammo, score, and wave counter
• Requires ARCore-compatible device (Samsung Galaxy S25, and most modern Android phones)
• No internet connection required — play anywhere
• No ads, no tracking, no microtransactions

**Recommended devices:**
• Samsung Galaxy S25 / S25+ / S25 Ultra (primary target)
• Google Pixel 6+ / Pixel 8+ / Pixel 9+
• Any Android 10+ device with ARCore support

**Important:**
• Requires a sufficiently lit environment for AR tracking
• Play in a clear, open space — you'll be moving around
• Hold your phone in landscape mode
• ARCore must be installed (auto-installs on supported devices from Play Store)
