AR Doom — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Overview

AR Doom is an augmented reality game that uses your device's camera to place game enemies in your real environment. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

Camera Access

AR Doom uses your device's camera exclusively for augmented reality gameplay. The camera feed is processed in real-time on your device to detect surfaces and track your position in space. Camera data is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared. The video feed is used only for rendering the game and is discarded immediately after each frame is drawn.

ARCore / Google Services

AR Doom uses Google's ARCore framework for augmented reality tracking. ARCore may collect anonymous spatial mapping data to improve AR quality. This data is handled by Google under their own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Collection

AR Doom does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your camera recordings or photos
  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Device identifiers

Local Data Storage

All game progress (high scores, wave reached, settings) is stored locally on your device. This data is never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

Advertising

AR Doom contains no advertisements. No ad SDKs are included in the app.

In-App Purchases

AR Doom has no in-app purchases. The game is completely free.

Network Access

AR Doom does not require an internet connection to play. No network requests are made during gameplay.

Permissions Used

  • Camera — Required for AR gameplay. Used in real-time, never stored.
  • Vibrate — Haptic feedback when firing weapons or taking damage.
  • Wake Lock — Keeps screen on during active gameplay.

Children's Privacy

AR Doom is rated Mature 17+ for fantasy violence. It is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date at the top.
